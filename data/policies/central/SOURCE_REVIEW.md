## Applicable document relationships

Read the base eligibility and buyer procedure with the later amendments. The 10 August 2026 notification updates the e-2W row, scheme outlay and terminal provisions. The 27 March 2026 e-rickshaw/e-cart row remains relevant in this packet, but that same page's earlier e-2W date and paragraph 46 require the August update. The latest amendment also records the L5 segment closure; the December letter supplies its earlier notice and target limitation.

Observed August source text distinguishes the scheme terminal date, claim-submission deadline and fund-exhaustion condition. It retains year-specific e-2W incentive rates and the lower-of-15%-of-ex-factory-price limitation. The e-rickshaw table has its own years, cap, price ceiling and target. None of this establishes that an individual vehicle/buyer qualifies or that the allocation remains unexhausted at purchase time.

Every draft page carries `state=Central`, `scheme=PM E-DRIVE` and the buyer scope. Required-update page links point to the March/August 2026 documents. Current rate pages link to base/guideline eligibility pages. Earlier rates or deadlines must not be used alone. Stage 6 must resolve these relationships during retrieval; Stage 4 only preserves and tests them.

The October 2025 letter grants a time-bound exception for specified historical invoicing/registration cases. It is audit-only and is not a blanket extension of current purchase eligibility or a current open claims window.
