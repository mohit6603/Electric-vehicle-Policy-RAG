## Reading the provisions together

The 2024 notification grants 100% road-tax and registration-fee exemption for the listed electric categories purchased and registered in Telangana, up to 31 December 2026 irrespective of the number registered. The old policy's quota-based tax/fee passages on page 10 are retained in audit data but excluded from answer context with seven hash-checked spans. The later notification and policy-period page are mandatory context. This does not create a cash purchase subsidy or establish remaining retrofit quota.

The policy's separate three-seater auto retrofit provision is retained: 15% of retrofit cost, capped at Rs.15,000 per vehicle, for the first 5,000 retrofits. The notification's lifetime bus language is limited to TSRTC-operated vehicles; it also qualifies industry-owned employee buses by exclusive employee use and no commercial use. Do not generalize lifetime exemption to all buses.

Charging page 11 encourages highway stations every 50 km and charging in existing townships with 1000+ families. These are not proof of installations or a numerical electricity tariff. Page 13's manufacturing highlights reference Electronics Policy 2016, which has not been collected in full. The mega-project threshold uses investment over Rs.200 crore **or** employment over 1000, not both. Preserve annual and cumulative SGST caps and the seven-year period. Policy investment/employment objectives are targets, not achieved results.
