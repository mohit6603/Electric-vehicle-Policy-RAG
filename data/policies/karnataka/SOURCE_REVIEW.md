## Reading the provisions together

The policy runs five years from its government order or until a new policy, with transition terms for approved projects. Page 26 supplies the period; pages 2 and 27–29 supply definitions, employment, sanction and transition conditions. Policy targets and station counts do not establish achieved outcomes or remaining slots.

Page 22 states a fast-charging capital subsidy of 25%, capped at Rs.10 lakh per station, for 500 stations covering 2W, 3W, cars and buses. It expressly gives no incentive for slow chargers. Swapping has separate vehicle-specific caps/counts; hydrogen is a different provision. Charging/manufacturing incentives are not a cash purchase subsidy for a private-car buyer.

Manufacturing tables require category and zone labels: general and special-category micro/small industries have different rates/caps. Medium enterprises have a separate four-installment provision. Large/mega/ultra-mega rates are 25% in Zone 1 and 20% in Zones 2/3; land is limited to 50 acres, with Bengaluru Urban/Rural land excluded, and disbursement is in five equal annual installments after commercial production. Industrial stamp-duty/registration concessions concern specified documents, not vehicle registration. Referenced Industrial Policy 2025–30, State MSME enabling orders and operating notifications have not been collected in full.

The old broad vehicle-tax exemption on policy page 12 is excluded from answer context, with the newer Act's EV tables required. The original audit text remains intact. **The Act requires a separate commencement notification; that notification has not been verified or collected.** Publication is not a verified effective date. The assistant can describe the Act's printed provisions but must not claim they establish current payable tax.

Part A5(a), physical page 6, prints new electric motor car/jeep/omnibus/private-service vehicle cost slabs: up to Rs.10 lakh, 5%; above Rs.10 lakh through Rs.25 lakh, 8%; above Rs.25 lakh, 10% of vehicle cost. Part A8(a) separately covers motor cabs and excludes specified out-of-State cabs with section 88(9) permits. Age-table percentages apply to the clause-A lifetime tax, not vehicle cost. Refund tables use different time boundaries. Preserve both the table and its continuation; do not infer universal tax exemption.

The registration-fee notification exempts only the rule 81 table serial 4 fees for issuing/renewing registration certificates and assigning a new registration mark to battery-operated vehicles. It does not exempt all charges or road tax. Its issuer is Central; inclusion in the Karnataka packet does not add a combined PM E-DRIVE query.

Appendix 2 prints zone totals **199/32/9**, but the AI table reading yields **198/33/9**, both totaling 240 taluks. The mismatch is unresolved. In particular, the page 35 image places Kurugodu in Zone 2; text extraction loses this column information. Do not move a taluk merely to match the printed totals. Team review must reconcile the rows and totals with applicable original/correcting instruments.
