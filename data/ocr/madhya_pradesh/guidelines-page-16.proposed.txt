Madhya Pradesh Electric Vehicle Policy Operational Guidelines 2025
Charging station incentives (labelled table reading):
Small charging stations: On chargers, capital subsidy of 30% of the value of charging equipment/machinery for the first 500 charging stations, maximum INR 1,50,000.
Medium charging stations: On chargers, capital subsidy of 30% of the value of charging equipment/machinery for the first 300 stations, maximum INR 3,00,000.
Large charging stations: On chargers, capital subsidy of 30% of the value of charging equipment/machinery for the first 200 stations, maximum INR 10,00,000.
Battery swapping stations: One-time capital subsidy on eligible fixed capital investment for service providers at 30%, for the first 300 swap stations in the State, maximum INR 5,00,000.

9.3.6 Site/premises photographs, required documents and certificates may be uploaded on the MP EV Tarang portal with application information.
