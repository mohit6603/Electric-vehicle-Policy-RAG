14.3 Conditions for NOC Approval 
14.3.1 The RWA cannot deny the NOC without a justified safety or retaliatory reason. 
14.3.2 If denied, the RWA must provide written reasons for rejection within the same 7-day period. 
14.4 Compliance & Monitoring 
14.4.1 RWAs must maintain a publicly accessible register of NOCs issued and denied. Residents 
can escalate delayed or unfairly denied requests through the MP EV Tarang Portal. [Refer 
to Annexure 17.]
