13.1 Government buildings will set a roadmap to setup charging or swapping stations in all of its parking

13.3 Inter-State Bus Terminals (ISBT), bus terminals and bus stops are encouraged to have charging

13.5 Existing buildings, including malls and other commercial properties, will be incentivized to install 
charging and battery-swapping stations. 
13.6 All new permits for commercial complexes. housing societies and residential townships with a
