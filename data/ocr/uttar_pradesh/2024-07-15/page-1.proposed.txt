Uttar Pradesh Government, Industrial Development Section 6, notification 29/2024/342/77-6-2024-6099/281/2022, dated 15 July 2024.
Amendment to the Uttar Pradesh Electric Vehicle Manufacturing and Mobility Policy 2022 notified on 14.10.2022.
Paragraph 4.3 subparagraph 2, revised provision:
Purchase subsidy as an early-bird incentive shall be provided in the defined segments at the following rates for five years from the policy notification date, namely 14.10.2022, or until the unutilised amount of Rs 403.97 crore is spent, whichever is earlier.
1. Two-wheeler electric vehicles: 15% of ex-factory cost, subject to a limit of Rs 5,000 per vehicle, a maximum outlay of Rs 100 crore and a maximum of 2 lakh electric vehicles.
