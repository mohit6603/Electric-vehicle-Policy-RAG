Office of the Transport Commissioner, Uttar Pradesh, Lucknow, dated 18 August 2025. Directions for implementation of EV purchase subsidy for aggregators, fleet operators and other operators.
The policy was notified on 14 October 2022. The Transport Department is the nodal department for EV purchase subsidy. An office order dated 19.02.2025 decentralised disbursement; only electric buses remain at Transport Commissioner headquarters level.
The paragraph 4.2(1) buyer definition refers to EVs purchased and registered in the state meeting the performance/efficiency specifications in Annexure II of the FAME-II notification dated 28 March 2019. Buyers include individuals, aggregators such as food-delivery/e-commerce logistics/couriers, and fleet operators including leasing companies, corporates, hotels and other operators.
Clarifications were sought on 27.03.2025; the HLEEVC meeting dated 23.04.2025 considered categories, eligibility documents and the effective date for hybrid vehicles. The directions follow on the next pages.
