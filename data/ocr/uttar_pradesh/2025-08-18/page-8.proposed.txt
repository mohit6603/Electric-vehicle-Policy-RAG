Checklist of documents for vehicle purchase subsidy.
Mandatory documents for qualification (left column):
- EV registration certificate (RC).
- GST registration.
- Evidence of bus/coach rental services with operator (contract, permit, declaration etc.).
- Evidence of tour-operator services (contract, permit, declaration etc.).
- Evidence of scheduled local bus/coach charter services (contract, permit, declaration etc.).
- Evidence of other travel arrangements/related services not elsewhere classified (contract, permit, declaration etc.).
- Company/firm incorporation certificate.
- Proof of commercial use, such as lease agreement, business registration or operation certificate.
Indicative eligibility-document checklist (right column):
- Incorporation/registration certificate.
- Business registration certificate (Udyam/MSME).
- GST registration, if applicable.
- Vehicle registration certificate (RC).
- FAME-II compliance certificate, if applicable.
- Proof of business use (contract, permit, declaration etc.).
- PAN and Aadhaar for persons/proprietors.
