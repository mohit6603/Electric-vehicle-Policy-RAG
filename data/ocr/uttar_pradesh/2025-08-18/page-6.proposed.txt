Government clarifications/directions under paragraph 4.2 of the UP EV Policy 2022:
Aggregators operate digital platforms to book, schedule and manage transport such as ride-sharing or delivery. They are registered with relevant authorities (MCA, GST etc.) and manage or own vehicle fleets.
Fleet operators are companies or persons operating fleets for passenger or goods transport under sole ownership or lease agreements and registered under applicable transport and tax laws.
Other operators include private/public institutions, government departments, startups or persons using EVs for operational or business purposes, outside the two categories above. Personal non-commercial use is not included in this other-operator category.
For strong hybrid electric vehicles (SHEV) and plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEV), the committee specified 11.07.2024, the date of the Infrastructure and Industrial Development Department order, as the effective date for implementation of purchase subsidy.
1. Aggregators: MoRTH issued Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines 2025 on 01.07.2025. UP state guidelines based on them were to be promulgated; separate directions would be issued.
2. Fleet and other operators: examine eligibility against definitions and documents approved in the 23.04.2025 HLEEVC meeting; continuation on the next page.
