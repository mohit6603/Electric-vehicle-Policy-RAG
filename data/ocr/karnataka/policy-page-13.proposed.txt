4.1.3 Supply Side Incentives and Concessions 
4.1.3.1 Incentives and Concessions for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises 
Capital subsidy for Micro Industries (VFA = Value of Fixed Assets).
General category: Zone 1: 30% of VFA, maximum INR 30 Lakh; Zone 2: 25%, maximum INR 25 Lakh; Zone 3: 20%, maximum INR 10 Lakh.
Special category (SC/ST, Women, Minorities, Physically Challenged and Ex-Servicemen): Zone 1: 35% of VFA, maximum INR 35 Lakh; Zone 2: 30%, maximum INR 30 Lakh; Zone 3: 25%, maximum INR 15 Lakh.
Capital subsidy for Small Industries (VFA = Value of Fixed Assets).
General category: Zone 1: 25% of VFA, maximum INR 200 Lakh; Zone 2: 20%, maximum INR 150 Lakh; Zone 3: 20%, maximum INR 50 Lakh.
Special category (same categories): Zone 1: 30% of VFA, maximum INR 225 Lakh; Zone 2: 25%, maximum INR 175 Lakh; Zone 3: 25%, maximum INR 75 Lakh.
Note on subsidy for Micro and Small Enterprises: The capital subsidy will be disbursed in 2 installments.
